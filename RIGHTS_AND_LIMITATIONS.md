# Distribution rights and evidential boundaries

## DOI and repository version

The authors supplied the Zenodo DOI **https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24168011** for an existing deposit. This package is a **locally updated repository archive** aligned with the manuscript title ending in *A Survey*. It has not been established that the currently published Zenodo files are byte-identical to this updated ZIP. Before citing this exact revised version, upload it as a new Zenodo version and verify its metadata and file contents. A DOI and public availability do not themselves establish copyright clearance.

## Copyright and licenses

The `licenses/` directory contains the MIT license notice (with a 2026 QCC research-team copyright statement) and the text of Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. These notices are retained **unchanged**. Their inclusion is not a determination that one license applies universally to the entire archive, nor that all contributors have individually approved a new license grant. The authors should reconcile the applicable rights and ownership of code, original research artifacts, documentation and jointly created outputs with the license stated in Zenodo's record. Third-party publications remain subject to their own rights.

The prior source audit omitted **13 complete original files** whose external distribution status was uncertain; those files remain excluded. The archive also excludes copyrighted full texts, licensed raw Scopus/Web of Science exports, confidential reviewer evidence, and the separate manuscript/figure PDF collection. Omitting restricted materials reduces redistribution exposure but is not a substitute for legal or institutional clearance of every derived item.

## Research-data boundaries

The catalogue contains bibliographic identity fields and research flags. `selection_traceability.xlsx` and `data/selection/` expose derived historical selection decisions for 343 unique candidates while intentionally leaving unavailable metadata for the 100 nonselected candidates blank. They do not replicate licensed database exports. The 243 selected records are not necessarily independent studies. The selected-paper catalogue and aggregate analyses can be checked with `make verify` and `make reproduce`, but no independent reassessment of the full texts, corpus-wide double extraction, or quantum simulation is claimed.

The 11 original scientifically validated PDF figures and the manuscript's approved disclosure statements remain in the **separate manuscript archive**. Only the two author-generated conceptual illustrations and their reproducible source are bundled here. The historical binary-PNG reproducibility limitation for other figures is not claimed to be resolved by this compact package.

Before uploading this **revised archive** as a new Zenodo version, check contributor approval, metadata and per-component license applicability. No new license or copyright holder has been assigned by this editorial update.
