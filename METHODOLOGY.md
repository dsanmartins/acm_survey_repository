# Screening, evidence and operational definitions

Initial title-and-abstract assessments were automated. The historical targeted review covered 40 records, with 33 recorded agreements and seven reconciled cases. The surviving record supports that historical accounting and final decisions; original separate individual human annotations are not available. Annotations subsequently supplied by the authors are not treated as proof that independent original decisions were recovered. No individual reviewer worksheet or identity-linked annotation is included here.

A separate initial full-text pilot covered 12 records. Its preserved aggregate matrix compares automated initial assessments with researcher assessments, with 11 agreements and Cohen's κ = 47/59. This is researcher–automated agreement in a small pilot, not agreement between two human reviewers, screening accuracy or reliability across the corpus. Reconciled final decisions are not used to calculate kappa. `make verify-kappa` retains the historical matrix and exact fractional result.

An eligibility clarification was documented on **November 5, 2026**, after the full-text screening round completed on October 9. It resolved one previously uncertain assessable record using unchanged evidence, without changing the 243 selected publications. The public selection trace preserves historical and effective decisions. The clarification is not represented as part of the initial eligibility protocol. Missing full texts and a bibliographic-identity issue remain distinct from scientific eligibility exclusions.

The category registry still has 86 categories. The 22 approved operational descriptions clarify existing definitions, inclusion, exclusion and limits. They do not add categories, rewrite the historical registry, recode an item or transfer memberships. Their complete text is in `docs/operational_category_definitions.tex` and `data/taxonomies/operational_definitions_22.csv`; the unified catalogue is in `docs/CATEGORY_DEFINITIONS.md`.

Evidence provenance is claim-specific: an illustrative claim is not converted retrospectively into an observation by a later execution; observation and repository origin may coexist. Analysis mode concerns the assessed mechanism, not simply whether a computer ran a checker. Assertions and invariants retain the source's predicate, scope and assumptions. None of these explanations authorizes a new inference for historical records.

The 58 RQ5 pairing proposals remain undecided and unapplied. The 45 family–level cells remain unmapped. Reproducing classifications and aggregates does not independently repeat source selection, extraction, an effectiveness evaluation or any original experiment.
