# Retrospective PRISMA 2020 reporting crosswalk

This document compares the **reporting already present** in the QCC manuscript and reviewer repository with PRISMA 2020. It is **not** a claim that a PRISMA-registered protocol was followed prospectively, that review conduct was assessed by PRISMA, or that the 243 publications are 243 independent studies.

The detailed **27-item checklist** is [`docs/PRISMA_2020_CROSSWALK.csv`](docs/PRISMA_2020_CROSSWALK.csv). Status values describe disclosure: Reported, Partially reported, Not performed, or Not applicable. In particular, no formal primary-study quality/risk-of-bias appraisal or certainty assessment was performed; no meta-analysis or intervention effect measure was calculated. These facts are not fixed by filling out a retrospective checklist.

PRISMA 2020 asks the selection flow to distinguish *records* identified, *reports* sought or unavailable, and *studies* included. The existing QCC Figure 1 is deliberately retained **without visual modification**. Its counts use the historical publication-record framework: 549 search occurrences, 343 unique candidate IDs, 256 full-text inputs, 253 records with eligibility assessed, 243 selected records, 10 full-text exclusions, and three unavailable/identity exceptions. Because overlapping publications may describe related investigations, a count of independent studies has **not** been established.

The new traceability workbook exposes effective decisions by candidate ID but does not supply complete bibliographic metadata for 100 nonselected candidates or licensed source exports. The diagram has not been falsely relabeled as fully PRISMA compliant. The only substantive crosswalk improvement is transparency about which items are supported and which remain incomplete.
